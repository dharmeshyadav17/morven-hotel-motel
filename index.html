A legendary highway stop since 1876.

Sitting proudly on the Warrego Highway at 78 Warrego Hwy, Morven QLD 4468, the Morven Hotel Motel blends old-world pub charm with clean, comfortable motel rooms. Whether you're crossing the outback to Charleville, heading to the coast, or chasing a classic country Sunday session — you'll be looked after like family.

Expect ice-cold drinks, generous pub feeds, spotless air-conditioned rooms with queen comfort, and sunsets that set the whole western sky on fire.

04 — About

Where Cobb & Co. once changed horses.

Morven grew around the Cobb & Co. staging post, and our hotel has been pouring beers for thirsty travellers since the 1870s. Today the Morven Hotel Motel at 78 Warrego Hwy pairs that frontier history — pressed-tin ceilings, wide verandas, yarns in the front bar — with properly comfortable motel accommodation out back.

Run by locals who love the west, we're the kind of place where the publican remembers your name, the steak is cooked how you asked, and someone will always help you back your van in.
